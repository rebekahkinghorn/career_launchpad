About this project

Built for the BYU IS Junior Core Case Competition to help incoming IS students discover career paths and prepare for internship interviews before the September STEM fair.

Salary figures are prototype estimates from general entry-level industry data. Before relying on them, cross-check against current Handshake and LinkedIn postings for BYU IS placements — that scrape is part of how this data should be validated going forward.
